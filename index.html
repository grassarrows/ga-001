DepthScan

A browser-based 3D capture tool. Live scanning uses the WebXR Depth Sensing API, available in Chrome on ARCore Android phones (Pixel, Galaxy, …). Points are fused into a voxel grid in real time and colored from the camera when the browser allows.

iPhone/iPad browsers don't expose LiDAR to the web yet. Capture with a native app (Polycam, 3D Scanner App, Scaniverse) and import the PLY / OBJ / GLB here to view, measure and convert.

Everything stays on this device (IndexedDB). Nothing is uploaded.
